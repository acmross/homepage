About

I'm a PhD student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the University at Buffalo, working with Dr. Wenyao Xu in the Embedded Sensing and Computing (ESC) Group.

Before starting my PhD, I spent several years as a biomedical product development engineer, working at the intersection of design, engineering, and health equity. I've led early-stage prototyping and clinical-needs work with UCSF surgeons, medical device teams, and university research groups, and I still take on freelance CAD and prototyping projects through my own practice, Across Consulting LLC. My background is interdisciplinary — a BA in Film & Digital Media, a BA in Visual Arts, and an MS in Biomedical Engineering — and that mix is what pulls me toward building technology that is as carefully designed for the people using it as it is technically sound.

Research

My PhD research is on AI-enabled wearable and mobile health systems in the ESC Group. I'm interested in embedded sensing, on-device AI, and bringing formal medical device design processes into research prototypes so that what we build can realistically move toward clinical use.

One project I contribute to is UltraSpeech, a real-time AI system for ultrasound tongue imaging in speech therapy, developed in the ESC Group in collaboration with speech-language pathology researchers at UB. The system tracks tongue movement during speech to give clinicians and patients real-time visual feedback, and is currently moving through clinical validation.
