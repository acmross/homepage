CUES - Three hackathons in 2026 - Fetch: 1st place, I led design and pitch - BiteBlocker: brain rot score, team met that afternoon - Baseline Drift: NASA Space Apps, daily voice check-in, you vs. your own baseline, still going - A weekend tells you if it's worth a semester SAY IT I also do hackathons. Three this year. Fetch is an AniTech project. It's AI that helps an animal rescue keep track of every case, from the first messy text message to adoption. It won first place at UB's AI for Good. I led the design and gave the pitch. BiteBlocker was at UB Hacking. It scores every video in your feed for brain rot, and you choose what happens: label it, blur it, or hide it. Our team of four met the afternoon the hackathon started. And Baseline Drift was our NASA Space Apps entry. It's a daily voice check-in for astronauts. It compares you to your own baseline and flags when something drifts, and it runs completely offline. We liked it so much we're still working on it. Why do I do these? Because a weekend is enough to find out if an idea is worth a semester. It's the fastest way I know to test something with real people. (~35 seconds)
