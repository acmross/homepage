Addresses: How my research supports teaching, learning and undergraduate involvement. CUES - One device, one user, one semester - I've already handed real projects to undergrads: Governor, K-Tree, NoLapse - Key skill is listening - "The more the merrier" SAY IT One thing I love about this kind of research is that undergrads can really do it. I want to give them the means to create something real. One device, one user, one semester. A sophomore can own that. And I've already done it. Two undergrads took over Governor's beak project. Two student engineers worked on the K-Tree design with me. And undergrads on my NoLapse team did real work on a real device. The key skill isn't advanced math. It's listening to people. Anyone can learn that. And we work around students' schedules, because a lot of them have jobs. The more the merrier, I say. (~35 seconds)
