At the top left is what I can teach right now. Intro programming and HCI. And two things most CS departments don't have: CAD and digital fabrication, and medical device design. I did those for a living. Below that are two I'm preparing to teach: data visualization, and physical computing and sensors. I'm not there yet, and I'd rather tell you that. I'd run it as sprints, with a hackathon as the midterm. And I'd be happy to run capstone. Real clients, real deadlines. I've done that work and I like it. (~50 seconds)
