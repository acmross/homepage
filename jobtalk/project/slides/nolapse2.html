From a preliminary device to a seamless one-piece

Phase I · Two-piece device. The joint failed under load.

Phase II · New four-piece mold. Seamless one-piece device.

Amanda Ross · Faculty Candidate Talk ·

Addresses: Overview of past research projects (NoLapse prototyping). CUES - Phase 1: preliminary devices to CAD to molds to a two-piece device - Failed in testing: glue not enough under load - Phase 2: better mold, updated design, seamless one piece - This loop is what I teach SAY IT This is my favorite part, the prototyping. Phase one. We took the preliminary devices that were used in emergency surgery and developed a CAD model. From that we created molds to make a two-piece device. And it had issues in testing. Medical-grade Loctite was not enough to keep the rod and ring pieces together under load. So, phase two. We improved the mold casting, updated the design after various testing, and that ultimately left us with a seamless one-piece device. Make it, test it, find out why it broke, and make it again. That loop is what I want my students to learn. (~35 seconds)
