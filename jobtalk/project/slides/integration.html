Amanda Ross · Faculty Candidate Talk ·

Addresses: How my teaching and research intersect. CUES - What I keep coming back to: being the bridge - Design and engineering; a technical team and the people who use it; a need and a working prototype - Same thing between research and teaching - Lab to class: real problems, real users - Class to lab: students become researchers SAY IT So how do my teaching and my research connect? I'm the bridge. That's what I keep coming back to in everything I do. I'm equal parts creative and analytical. I've been the bridge between design and engineering, between a technical team and the people who'll actually use what they build, and between a need and a working prototype. And it's the same thing here, between research and teaching. Going one way, real problems and real users from my research go straight into my classroom. My students don't build for a grade. They build for a real person. Going the other way, those students come back as researchers. That's what happened with Governor's beak, and with the K-Tree. So it's not two jobs for me. It's one job, and I'm standing in the middle of it. (~45 seconds)
