Addresses: Research statement, the motivation behind my research. CUES - Ranger, family dog, I grew up with him - 12 years old, arthritis, couldn't bend his legs, fell asleep standing up - I made him a dog hammock - Not elegant, but it worked - Why biomedical engineering, with animals in mind - Same idea in everything since: one user, one need SAY IT Before I get to the projects, let me tell you about this guy. This was Ranger. He was most definitely part of our family. I grew up with him. And one summer I came home, and he was suddenly this brittle twelve-year-old with really bad arthritis. He couldn't bend his legs, and he'd fall asleep standing up. I couldn't just stand by and watch him suffer. So I made him a sort of dog hammock, out of PVC pipe. And for the rest of his days he was much more comfortable. I realize it's not the most elegant device. But it's the reason I got a master's in biomedical engineering, and it's why I care so much about helping animals and people. You'll see that same idea in every project after this. One user, one real need, and you build for them. (~35 seconds)
