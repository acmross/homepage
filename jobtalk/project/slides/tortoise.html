He's a tortoise, and his beak broke and grew in wrong, so he couldn't eat right. We needed a device that would push his bottom beak over so the top one could grow back in. And his caretaker, Larry, had to be able to take it on and off for feedings. We literally tried everything. Rubber bands, weird clips. We settled on gluing on a base piece and attaching the rest with magnets. I made over 50 CAD designs and did more than 30 fittings with him. That's him getting 3D scanned in the middle photo. And then I passed the project on to two undergrads, who measure his progress every week. I call the final device Gov's horn. (~40 seconds)
